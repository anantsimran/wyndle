# Character artwork

Original generated illustrations made with the built-in imagegen tool for this
project. These are unofficial interpretations, not licensed or official artwork.
Characters and literary inspiration belong to Brandon Sanderson.

The runtime uses `dalinar.jpg` (hero) and `kaladin.jpg` (focus/rest companion).
Generated PNGs were resized and encoded as JPEG for local web delivery. Artwork
is bundled; no external request is made by the dashboard.

To replace a character image, keep its filename or update both `index.html` and
the asset allowlist in `commands/ui.py`. Keep alt text descriptive. The CSS uses
`object-position` to keep faces visible at sidebar widths.

## Final prompts

### Dalinar

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original character artwork for Wyndle, a calm personal productivity web app inspired by The Stormlight Archive. Primary request: portray Dalinar Kholin, a mature Alethi man with warm tan skin, strong weathered features, short dark hair graying at the temples, in deep blue military coat with understated gold trim, standing in quiet resolve on a rocky terrace above a clouded landscape. Half-body portrait, turned slightly toward viewer's left, one relaxed hand resting on a stone parapet. Style: refined original painterly fantasy editorial illustration, natural proportions, subtle canvas grain, soft brushwork, beautiful restrained detail, not anime, not a copy of an existing cover. Mood: grounded, compassionate strength, warm dawn rim light cutting through storm blue clouds, a new beginning. Color palette: storm blue, slate navy, muted brass gold, pale luminous blue atmosphere. Composition: landscape 3:2, character on right two-thirds, face clear, environment softly fades into mist on the left; meant to sit beside a headline. No lettering, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no borders. Do not draw other characters or weapons in action.

### Kaladin

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original character artwork for Wyndle, a calm personal productivity web app inspired by The Stormlight Archive. Primary request: portray Kaladin Stormblessed, a young adult Alethi man with warm tan skin, shoulder-length wavy black hair, dark eyes, subtle forehead scars, in a deep blue military coat, holding a spear loosely at his side, quietly watching an open sky after a storm. A tiny graceful blue spren light curls nearby like a ribbon of wind. Half-body portrait with caring, reflective expression, not triumphant or aggressive. Style: refined original painterly fantasy editorial illustration, natural proportions, subtle canvas grain, soft brushwork, beautiful restrained detail, not anime, not a copy of an existing cover. Mood: protective, resilient, room to breathe; luminous pale blue stormlight and a little warm dawn light. Color palette: storm blue, slate navy, muted brass gold, pale luminous blue atmosphere. Composition: landscape 3:2, character on left half, soft mist and space on right, clear face; useful in a small focus/rest companion card. No lettering, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no borders. No additional human characters, no battle or action pose.
